  2. Spread them in one layer next to the grading mat marker.
  3. Shoot from directly above in good light.

How grading works

  1. Detect: an AI model (YOLO, trained on 2,600 labelled onion images) finds every bulb and marks rot, mould, sprouting and split/double bulbs.
  2. Measure: the printed marker of known size converts pixels to millimetres, so undersized bulbs (< mm) are measured, not guessed.
  3. Cut test: cut faces are checked for dark/brown tissue to catch internal rot that is invisible from outside.
  4. Grade: Grade A % = sound bulbs of full size, adjusted for the internal-rot rate. The rest is URS. A lot with ≥ % Grade A is accepted as FAQ.
  5. Report: a PDF with photos, GPS, time and a tamper-proof fingerprint + QR code for verification.

Thresholds follow the team's reading of AGMARK/NAFED norms and are configurable.
